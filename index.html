Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about Hydrew.

Hydrew is the "Ultimate Liquid Utility NFT," a next-generation, dynamically evolving NFT built on the blockchain. It bridges the gap between digital ownership and real-world sustainability, inspired by the synergy of raw nature (Water) and sleek engineering (The Shield).
The concept is inspired by a story featuring Dewey, a hyperactive water droplet, and Hydro, a sleek stainless steel water bottle. Initially at odds, they realize they need each other: Dewey represents life and hydration, while Hydro provides the protective "armor" to keep him safe, cool, and useful.
Water is the foundation of life—the human body is approximately 60% water. It is essential for transporting nutrients, removing waste, and regulating temperature. Because humans cannot store water like fat, it is our most immediate daily biological dependency.
It depends on age and lifestyle. Generally, adult men need about 13 cups (~3.0 Litres) daily, and adult women need about 9 cups (~2.2 Litres). Pregnant women need 10 cups, and breastfeeding women require up to 13 cups.
Modern lifestyles make it hard to access clean water on the go, often leading to dehydration or reliance on single-use plastic bottles. Hydrew promotes the use of reusable water bottles to encourage regular hydration, provide convenience, and reduce environmental pollution.
It is not just "boring static pixel art." Hydrew is a dynamically evolving NFT that represents a movement toward a healthier lifestyle and environmental responsibility, connecting a biological necessity with digital ownership.
